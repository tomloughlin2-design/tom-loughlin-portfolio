Case Study

Rebuilding Viator's Mobile App into Its Highest-Converting Surface

Viator's app was close to being pulled from Apple and Google Play. I took over leadership, made the case for full reinvestment, and led the design and product transformation that turned it into the company's highest-converting surface.

Role

Design & Product Leadership

Company

Viator

Outcome

Highest-Converting Surface

The Challenge

Viator's mobile app was critically broken - functionally behind the web, riddled with unresolved bugs, and at risk of being removed from both the Apple App Store and Google Play. App development had been deprioritized for years, with most features built for web only. The result was a fragmented, frustrating experience that was losing users and failing the business.

My Role

I took over leadership of the mobile experience and became the driving force behind Viator's strategic shift to make the app its core B2C surface. This meant building organizational buy-in, restructuring cross-functional teams, defining the multi-phase roadmap, and setting the design and product direction.

The Approach

Phase 1

True Basics

Fixed critical bugs, closed parity gaps with the web, and addressed core UX issues across login, search, and booking flows.

Phase 2

Elevate the Core

Introduced new capabilities including maps, improved search, richer media, and in-destination features.

Phase 3

World Class

Positioned the app as the end-to-end travel companion with AI search, logistics, and a differentiated experience.

The Outcome

25%

of booking revenue

40%

higher conversion than mobile web

1.94 vs 1.28

items per trip compared to mobile web

The app shifted from a business risk to Viator's most strategically important customer surface. It not only outperformed mobile web on conversion, but also increased trip depth and long-term customer value. Most importantly, it reset organizational confidence in what mobile could drive when product and design were treated as core growth levers.
